Elections

The Elections service provides citizens with secure and convenient access to the Xephortian Republic’s electoral process. Elections are conducted entirely online through the official Electoral Portal, allowing eligible citizens to participate remotely in elections, cast their votes, and access relevant electoral information.

The service is designed to make voting accessible, efficient, and transparent while enabling the Government to administer the electoral process digitally.
