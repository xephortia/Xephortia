Legislation

The Legislation service provides public access to the laws of the Xephortian Republic. Citizens, residents, businesses, and visitors may browse the official Gazette, view bills and acts voted on by the National Council, Executive Decrees, regulations, and other legislative instruments published by the Government.

All legislation is available online through the official Xephortian legislation folder, ensuring transparency, accessibility, and public access to the nation's laws.
