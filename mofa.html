Foreign Affairs

The Foreign Affairs service provides access to diplomatic relations information and consular services. Foreign relations are strictly only possible through an official treaty signed by the Xephortian Government, and through its ratification by the Xephortian Gazette.

The service also supports diplomats and authorized representatives seeking to establish diplomatic relations, establish or manage embassies, and handle other foreign affairs matters. All applications and procedures relating to diplomatic protection, passports, diplomatic relations, embassies, and related foreign affairs services are conducted through the official Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
