Xephortia ID

The Phortal ID number is a unique identification number issued by the Xephortian Government to citizens and sponsored residents (locally employed workers who are not full citizens). It serves as the official legal identifier for individuals in the Xephortian Republic.

Your Phortal ID number is required to access a wide range of government services, including healthcare, education, banking, taxation, licensing, elections (where eligible), and other public services. Eligibility for certain government services may vary depending on your legal residency status, and some services are available exclusively to citizens.

The Phortal ID number appears on the following official government documents: Xephortia ID Card This service allows eligible citizens and sponsored residents to apply and renew an expired Civil ID card or request a replacement card if it has been lost, stolen, or damaged.

Digital ID preferred

The Xephortian Government encourages citizens and sponsored residents to use their Digital Identity Card, which is available through the Xephortia eGov Portal. A physical Civil ID card is generally unnecessary for most government and public services and should only be requested when specifically required or preferred by the holder.
